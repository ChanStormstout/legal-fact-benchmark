Did Wazir Narain Singh have the power to grant a permanent lease of the mortgaged property prior to the 1929 amendment of the Transfer of Property Act? [O1]
Wazir Narain Singh did not possess an inherent common law power to grant a permanent lease binding on the mortgagee prior to the 1929 enactment of Section 65-A; instead, the High Court applied Section 66, determining the lease valid only because it did not render the Bank's security insufficient, a finding the Privy Council accepted as the sole surviving legal question.
