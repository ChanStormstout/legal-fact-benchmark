# 1251841: what the metrics change in the interpretation

This is one exposed development dispute, evaluated against permitted appellate
excerpts and law. The ultimate target reasoning and disposition are withheld.
The reference has 17 important content requirements; it is conditional on that view,
not the true final outcome of the litigation. All source and report bytes remain
available in the package. An attributed High Court finding can be source-supported
without being independently legally correct.

## Correct important content, not topic mention

A0 earns seven items: the earlier Bank mortgage; the four-village 1925 Patta and
relative sequence; Chohan's earlier representation finding; the High Court's
rejection of constructive res judicata; the High Court's section 66 approach; its
no-insufficiency finding; and the later insertion of section 65-A. It nevertheless
confuses the original Bank with the later claimant and reverses the High Court's
possession disposition. A correct earlier sentence cannot recover the corresponding
item when an unqualified later sentence contradicts it.

The candidate correctly recovers four items: the defendants' attributed Patta-survival
objection, the prior High Court section 66 approach and no-insufficiency finding,
and the statutory timing. Its Chohan sentence has correct narrower content but
explicitly assigns later nonchallenge to the Privy Council without source support;
the required full procedural relation does not earn a point. The supplied record
reports an earlier appeal admitted there, followed by a later judgment saying
“before us”; it does not authorize equating the two stages.

Delivered C retains the latter three items and loses the defendants' objection.
A heading asking whether the lease binds, or a system qualification error message,
is not a substantive answer. UNKNOWN from an internal task is not evidence that the
original judgment lacks a clear prior finding.

## Fewer errors is an improvement, with a measured cost

Delivered C has two confirmed serious events against four in A0. It avoids A0's
explicit claimant-Bank conflation and reversed High Court possession outcome in the
surviving delivery. This is an observed reduction, even though errors remain.
It still asserts unsupported pre-1929 legal incapacity and invents later appellate
acceptance/nonreview. All three outputs therefore have a serious-error case
indicator of one and a materially overreaching main answer.

R falls from A0 7/17 to delivered C 3/17, a difference of **−23.5 percentage points**.
P falls from 24/34 to 8/12 (70.6% to 66.7%). H falls from 52.0 to 27.9. Thus C does
not dominate A0 on this case. Calling A0 completely correct would also be wrong:
its main outcome is seriously reversed. The separate component scores explain both
statements without forcing an arbitrary single winner.

The candidate-to-delivery removal retains three of four correct requirements, and
8 of 17 supported assertions. The mapping in `web-final-evaluation/comparison.json`
traces each retained assertion. The lost correct assertions include plaintiff
subrogation, the defendants' objection, the reported High Court dismissal and
material events. They were present in candidate prose; they did not all earn a
complete reference item because the requirements also check attribution and
necessary context. Removal reduces unsupported/contradicted assertions from 15 to
4, but is not a demonstrated successful correction of their text. Three candidate
assertions remain annotation-uncertain and none survives delivery.

## What is and is not established

The new Web grader produced 51 item decisions and 81 assertion records. Exact
quotes and hashes pass mechanical validation. Nonblind source adjudication changed
three coverage labels, two assertion labels, one logical paraphrase and one serious
error explanation, preserving every original decision. No reference item changed
after grading. The assertion atomization and severity judgments are still model
assessments, not expert gold. The separate 12-item Codex reference demonstrates
reference sensitivity but is not interchangeable with this primary reference.

The trace supports the diagnosis that filtering can remove useful content while
leaving serious unsupported content. It does not isolate the causal contribution
of each C module or prove that removing all program checking would improve quality.
The source-extraction, false-pass and recovery problems need separate method
choices, evaluated on delivered R, P, serious events, main answer and cost under a
new frozen protocol. No new method is implemented or run by this publication.

There is no independent-dispute sample for a confidence interval or generalization
claim. No historical source is used in these final reports, so historical RAG has
no demonstrated final-answer benefit here. A0 remains one ordinary call; evaluator
annotations did not enter either original experiment.
