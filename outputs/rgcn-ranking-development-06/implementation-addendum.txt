Input isolation: when review excludes a fact or relation, dependent candidate links also leave the active input. Do not remove dependencies silently and continue counting coverage. When an authority link is excluded, conservatively mask its unit-level scope as UNKNOWN and retain the original scope separately, preventing incorrect scope from returning through summary features. This mask may discard correct scope information and is not a semantic correction. The frozen training snapshot already contained this implementation; this note only explains it and does not change the method.
