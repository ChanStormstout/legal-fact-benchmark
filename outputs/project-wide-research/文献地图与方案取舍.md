## How nearest work changed the proposal

Expert definition revision and downstream-impact review initially looked promising as broad contributions. [ClassMATe](https://aclanthology.org/2025.tacl-1.55/) and query-guided review make those broad claims insufficient. A set objective also falls within established robust optimization.

This changes the evidence order: identify recurring source errors and damaged tasks, test existing methods under fair conditions, then propose a specific mechanism only for a stable remaining gap. If none remains, use existing algorithms and study representation necessity and limits.

## Selected emphasis

A is the main research task: independently evaluate source-supported distinctions and cross-case reuse against expert schema, adapted EDC, hybrid and source/local methods. B begins with short queries, witnesses, fixed-denominator support and existing compact selection; add uncertainty, joint selection and budgeted review separately. C tests limited application through reviewed authorities, explicit premises, support/opposition and unresolved conditions. Statistical patterns never become authorities.

The [complete design](算法研究方案.md) gives algorithms, pseudocode, rationale and rejection criteria. It is implementable research planning, not demonstrated corpus effectiveness. Historical comparator proposals do not authorize changing the current plain-LLM baseline.
