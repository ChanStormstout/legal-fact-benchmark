# Independent Literature Verification

Historical verification date: September 29, 2026. Main window: September 29, 2024–September 29, 2026, with earlier foundations. This records sources actually opened by Codex and reading scope, separately from Pro's self-reported reading. No experiments were reproduced. Paper results are not verified project results. Translation does not constitute a fresh citation audit.

Method/experiment reading means the listed sections, not every appendix or proof. Abstract-only reading cannot establish detailed mechanisms or results. Preprint and publication versions of one paper are not counted separately.

## Findings Affecting the Design

Fact canonicalization, legal factors, LLM+ILP, legal KGs and checkable symbolic reasoning have clear precedents. Historical comparator proposals should include these; changing the domain does not establish algorithmic novelty. This historical recommendation does not override current authorization for a minimal direct-call baseline.

A needs semantic-error/coverage evaluation, B binding correctness, replication and incremental value, and C normative applicability/support-chain correctness. One shared win/loss accuracy cannot replace them. Facts generated from full judgments, target-cited precedents and same-case nodes may contain unavailable prediction-time information. Task timing, not the field name facts, determines admissibility.

Query-guided cleaning, possible worlds and budgeted review are close precedents. Compare established review plus the same selector before attributing gains to a new mechanism. Robust set optimization is established; prioritize A's abstraction/independent reuse evaluation and treat complex B selection as an experimental variant.

The contribution remains a hypothesis: preserving legally important distinctions in traceable representations may improve patterns and rule application. If simple schemas or existing methods suffice, accept that result and reduce complexity.
