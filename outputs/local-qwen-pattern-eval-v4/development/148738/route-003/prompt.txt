[p0006.s006] This fact regarding sub-letting to Sujoy Kumar Das Gupta came to the notice of the landlord respondent for the first time on 15th September, 1975 when Sujoy Kumar Das Gupta, the Sub lessee himself sent a notice through his Solicitor intimating to the respondent landlords that partnership between Shri Gupta and appellant had been dissolved.
