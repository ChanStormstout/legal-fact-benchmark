# Core Algorithm Design: Eight-Slide Speaker Script

Historical proposal, September 29, 2026. Approximately 12 minutes. Examples and rules are fictional; real-data superiority is unverified.

## 01 Three Algorithms and Their Relationship

The design retains evidence, facts, patterns, factors, elements and conclusions. A gives facts explicit meanings and computational uses; B proposes reusable relations and executes/checks/repairs them; C acquires sourced rules/bridges and reconstructs arguments. Point to A-to-C: B is not the only legal-fact entry. The original PDF already has embedding retrieval, LLM relation judgments, conservative clustering and provenance. Additions concern definition revision, object-relation search and cross-module use conditions. Judgment data support retrospective discovery, not predecision prediction. All examples/rules in this deck are fictional and no real-case effectiveness result is established.

References: Original PDF pp1–8; team pipeline and assignments; 最终算法方案.md §§1–3.

## 02 A: Normalizing Different Expressions

Begin with sourced assertions, open extraction and cards containing parameters, exclusions, positives and hard boundaries. Retrieve by aliases, BM25 and vectors, check each slot against sources, and accept a uniquely supported most-specific machine mapping. Similarity cannot offset important qualifications. Payment examples share a type while retaining debt, partial/full settlement and court-found/alleged status. Strict equivalence requires full signatures under a declared abstraction policy; families organize related/parent types without substitution. Dispatch and receipt may be separate NOTICE_EVENT types. Repair aliases/parameters or split definitions from sources and replay reviewed positives/negatives without regression before release. Keep source status, field availability, machine acceptance and human review separate: COURT_FOUND may still be unreviewed extraction. ClassMATe motivates definition/instance revision, GraphRefine distinguishes correction, meaning-preserving rewrite and exclusion; their supervised scores do not transfer automatically.

References: ClassMATe https://aclanthology.org/2025.tacl-1.55.pdf; GraphRefine https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1353.pdf; Final design §§3–4.

## 03 A: Eligibility Depends on Use

Initial extraction finds payment and full amount but misses only for D2. Metadata preservation alone lets downstream full=true incorrectly discharge D1. Attach the residual to its assertion and affected debt/settlement-target fields. Queries declare required reads and the compiler closes dependencies; intersecting unresolved residuals block that use. An approved projection may report payment occurrence without proving D1 settlement. Unknown residual scope blocks its assertion or affected original group; an unsupported LLM irrelevance claim cannot unblock it. Source review resolving D2 produces a definite mismatch. Independent complete D1 payment remains usable, avoiding whole-case blockage. Dependency analysis can err, so random audits sample source paragraphs including omissions, exclusions and high-confidence records. Initial review allocation is one quarter random and three quarters conflict/boundary, not a theoretical optimum. Purpose-level blocking/source repair is the primary hypothesis.

References: Final design §4; source-grounded use-dependency design is a proposed mechanism, not an established performance result. All text is hypothetical.

## 04 B: Proposing and Repairing Patterns

Start with real connected 2–4-atom fragments, not arbitrary predicate combinations. Connections require actual parties/contracts/events; case_id, equal amounts or types do not suffice. Typed anti-unification preserves common relations. LLMs add sourced descriptions and distinguish missing observations from missing operators. Fix each candidate's intent. Here same-debt settlement after receipt is violated by joining D1 notice and D2 payment. Node binding traces diagnose ununified debt variables for evidenced local repair. Also require receipt rather than dispatch, full settlement of that debt and eligible status; dates must belong to the corresponding events. Separate intent beams prevent broad concepts winning by coverage. Initial limits: six expanded atoms, six object variables, four scalar variables, two levels, three rounds/intent, eight patches/parent, beam twelve; reserve 10% execution budget for short two-atom supplements. After source/scope/semantic checks compare simplicity and cost. Separate unknown fields, failed bindings, no recorded witness and timeout. Silence is not real-world falsity. RHDA/ADVENT motivate execution feedback/language acquisition, but their supervision differs.

References: RHDA https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/eeffa70bcbbd43f6bd067edebc6595e8-Paper-Conference.pdf; ADVENT https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01585v1; language bias https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21486v2; Final design §5.

## 05 B: Relation Library versus Compression Dictionary

G retains executable relations and review grades; S is a compact overview selected from G. C can use A/G directly, so rare exceptions survive exclusion from S. MDL charges a definition once and each call's real bindings, retaining residual facts. Encode full definitions, all reconstruction variables, source IDs, statuses and residuals; a short P name cannot manufacture savings. Fix D to one A version and charge a shared header consistently. Each fact record is reconstructed once in the initial cover, though calls may share contracts/parties and G witnesses may overlap. This simpler choice may lose compression versus overlapping tokens. Inner covering starts from literals and chooses greatest legal savings; outer add/delete moves recompute total length and require at least one bit improvement. The first call need not repay the entire definition. No MILP or global optimality claim. Every accepted change round-trips D exactly. KG-MDL offers related shared-node/nonoverlapping-edge coding; Snor requires a special overlap mechanism, not merely deleting disjointness. No measured lengths are shown.

References: KG-MDL https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12908; Snor https://eda.rg.cispa.io/pubs/2025/snor-vanleeuwen%2Cvreeken.pdf; Final design §6.

## 06 C: Acquiring Rules and Bridges

C starts with legal-source packages, not reliable ready-made rules: text, definitions, exceptions, references, jurisdiction, validity period, procedure and judicial use. Follow identified references and mark missing provisions without claiming exhaustive law retrieval. Jointly draft RuleCard/BridgeCard using shared vocabulary and source every component. Bridges establish what actual receipt additionally needs, such as designated recipient and debt identification under the fictional rule. Deemed receipt is a separate legal rule, never an actual-receipt fact. Type/binding checks and independently source-derived premise/exception/scope tests guide local repair. Self-authored rules and answers cannot self-certify. Allow three interpretations with three rounds each; preserve unresolved disputes. Legal review approves normative meanings, bridges, exception policies and priorities for formal use. Mere factors remain ReasonFor/Against rather than sufficient victory conditions. DDL motivates joint acquisition/source tests; LRI/SILVER induction proposes candidates, not legal authority from majority support.

References: DDL https://arxiv.org/html/2506.08899v3; LRI/SILVER https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14104v2; AJF https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.128.pdf; Final design §7.

## 07 A Worked Final Output

Use Alpha K1/D1 with independent complete p5, not D2-qualified p1. n1 arrives May 4 and p5 settles May 13, giving nine calendar days. Dispatch on May 1 would give twelve and cannot replace receipt. Assume recipient/debt bridges hold but legal revocation is unspecified. If qualified revocation evidence attacks a default rule, default support can stand while revocation remains unknown; absence is not proven. A policy requiring explicit nonrevocation instead leaves a missing premise. C first constructs the complete finite arguments/attacks, then grounded labels: undefeated arguments IN, those defeated by IN OUT, others possibly unresolved. Do not require exceptions already IN before drawing attacks or certify incomplete graphs. Restricted ASPIC+ handles explicit priorities and unique last-defeasible-rule comparison; unsupported complex norms remain documented. K2/D3 lacks receipt timing; partial payments/allegations cannot substitute for complete court findings. Output concerns a particular termination ground, not whole-case victory.

References: ASPIC+ tutorial https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/19462166.2013.869766; Final design §§7–8. Rule, dates and cases are hypothetical.

## 08 First Implementation and Research Test

Implement A/B/C for one issue/stage. Focus on purpose-level blocking and local source repair: a fact may qualify for one use and remain pending for another. GraphRefine repairs facts, ClassMATe definitions, RHDA/ADVENT programs, and dependency propagation is established. Combining them is insufficient; demonstrate incremental value for legal meaning, objects, status and deadlines. At matched model/source/expert budget compare ignoring qualifications, whole-assertion blocking and dependency-level blocking/repair on misuse, correct witnesses retained, pending rate and review time. Reject-all cannot win on precision. Fall back to assertion blocking if local scope is unreliable; retain real seeds/deterministic execution if LLM edits add no benefit. Defer fine-tuning, all-member EQ/MILP, broad enumeration, overlapping-record coding, possible worlds and automatic factor weights. Preserve outcome association after party/request/stage label validation: compare single facts versus added relations within dispute-group training splits, without final-confirmation repairs. Present an evidence-motivated, testable design, not superiority or established novelty.

References: Final design §§9–10; recent-paper coverage and choices in 文献依据与最终取舍.md. No real-case experiment has been run for this design.
