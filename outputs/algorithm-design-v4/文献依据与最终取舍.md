## Search and Reading Scope

The main window is late 2024 through September 29, 2026, with directly relevant earlier foundations. Topics include open extraction/canonicalization, active clustering/category discovery, definition revision/pattern induction, predicate invention/program learning, constrained subgroup discovery, legal factors/rule acquisition/nonmonotonic argumentation and legal knowledge graphs. Sources include original publication sites, ACL Anthology, NeurIPS/ICLR/IJCAI/PMLR, arXiv, author pages and reference chaining. Reddit and secondary summaries did not support method selection.

This is targeted cross-field research, not a preregistered systematic review or exhaustive coverage of two years. ## Additional Checks of Established Methods

- [KG-MDL](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12908): reread Section 4 representation, coding, search and cover restrictions. Shared nodes/nonoverlapping edges are a graph-coding choice, not an existing encoder for our multifield assertions. Version one uses nonoverlapping records with shared parties/contracts, without claiming a new compression principle.
- [Snor](https://eda.rg.cispa.io/pubs/2025/snor-vanleeuwen%2Cvreeken.pdf): read Sections 3–5 coding/cover/search and Section 6 comparisons. Overlap requires a newly-covered-item mechanism, not merely removal of disjointness. Gains are not universal. Complex overlap is deferred, remaining an evidence-based alternative if simple coding proves insufficient.
- [ASPIC+ tutorial](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/19462166.2013.869766): rechecked Section 3.5 last-link, attack/defeat and Section 4 consistency conditions. Last defeasible rules may form a set, not the last executed step. A restricted fragment cannot inherit every guarantee of the full framework.

These are implementation checks of established mechanisms, not newly published work. Recent papers affect acquisition, definition revision and feedback; joins, coding and argumentation retain established explicit semantics.

## Decisions After Independent Design and Pro Review

Pro returned a complete response after approximately 22 minutes, distinguishing its own reading from attached reading records. Full request/reply are saved. A displayed search count does not establish equivalent deep paper reading.

| Decision | Rationale and limit |
|---|---|
| Purpose-dependent blocking in A | Addresses preserved qualifications being ignored in computation. Default scope is conservative and requires reviewed sources or approved rules to narrow. Formalization does not guarantee semantic correctness. |
| Random audits sample source paragraphs | Auditing only extracted facts misses zero-extraction, excluded and high-confidence systematic errors. |
| Real fragments lead B search; repair each intent separately | Prevents broad concepts winning on coverage. Bounded short-query supplementation is not complete-search assurance. |
| S uses a decodable nonoverlapping-record approximation | Withdraws the prior overlap-token and v2 MILP default recommendations. Focus complexity on semantic repair; G retains eligible relations so compression does not become legal filtering. |
| C treats unknown exceptions by each rule's policy | Corrects Pro's earlier universal-gate suggestion. Default IN arguments and unknown exception facts may coexist, with explicit default dependence. |
| One falsifiable contribution hypothesis | Test whether purpose-level blocking/source repair retains more correct witnesses at matched budget. Dependency propagation and LLM checking alone are not novelty claims. |

No extra agreement-implies-correctness round is added. This response addresses the key disagreements, and classical semantics were separately checked. Actual legal interpretation and project advantage still need independent review and experiments.

## Selection

A uses definition-constrained open category induction and versioned revision. B uses sourced relational seeds, bounded concept proposals and local program repair, separating descriptive discovery from supervised association learning. C uses sourced rule cards, bridge requirements, independent translation tests and traceable arguments. Exact relational execution remains because bindings and counting require explicit semantics, not because classical algorithms are inherently better.

Recent work changes candidate acquisition, error localization, category revision and feedback use. A full model may be unsuitable because its supervision is unavailable or its outputs cannot express this task; task differences do not invalidate all its mechanisms. Each added component must address a specific failure, rather than adding every interesting module at once.
