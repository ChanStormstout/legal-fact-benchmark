# Legal AI Core Algorithms: Final Recommended Design

September 29, 2026. This implementation proposal follows independent literature research and complete ChatGPT Pro discussions. It supersedes the default v2/v3 proposals. It has not been implemented or shown superior on real legal data. The meeting slides use the same algorithms, example and terminology. This historical proposal does not authorize new execution.

## 1. Objective and Selection

The project aims to compare facts reliably across cases and trace how they support legal arguments. The overall sequence remains evidence/documentary statements → canonical facts → fact combinations → factors/subtests → elements → claims and defenses → issues → conclusions. Legal sources and rules independently determine the standards of legal judgment.

| Algorithm | Problem | Selected approach | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: fact normalization | Share explicit meanings across expressions while retaining important distinctions | Versioned definition-card mapping, purpose-specific qualification checks, source-supported definition/instance repair | Locate errors in fields, meanings or use conditions rather than expanding categories by similarity or chained merges |
| B: relational-pattern discovery | Find reusable combinations with correct object relationships | Generalize real connected fragments, supplement with sourced LLM proposals, execute/check/locally repair under fixed meanings | Start with witnesses and expose failed joins/conditions rather than only a good/bad verdict |
| C: legal connection | Determine what descriptive facts establish under exceptions and counterarguments | Jointly obtain rules and bridges from legal-source packages, independently test/review them, then construct a finite argument graph | Check both rule translation and application while retaining defaults, objections and missing premises |

C reads eligible A facts directly; B supplies reusable relations and retrieval assistance. Infrequent facts/exceptions or those that do not compress remain available to C. B patterns are not legal rules.

The first mechanism to investigate is purpose-specific permission/blocking of factual use with source-supported local repair. New compression formulas and argument engines are not claimed as this project's innovation.

## 2. Existing Materials and Evidence Limits

The original PDF already specifies atomic extraction, role normalization, predicate arguments, context filtering, embedding retrieval, six semantic relations, conservative merging, definition/representative/boundary checks, provenance and change logs. It hides outcomes during normalization, later mines outcome-related patterns, and names FP-Growth, gSpan and ANCO-HITS. This proposal adds executable maintenance, search and repair; the source does not merely contain vague ideas.

Structural inspection found 6,954 JSONL document records: 6,418 status=ok, 497 empty, 39 error, and 5,424 with nonempty facts. These counts do not establish correctness. Twenty inspected samples mix domains and contain segmentation errors. Different doc_id values do not prove independent disputes. Start with one issue and procedural stage, verify source paragraphs and group related documents.

Reliable outcome labels, a complete rule library and expert fact-equivalence references are unavailable. Version one supports retrospective description and argument reconstruction. Outcome association comes only after validating party–request–stage–disposition labels. Postjudgment findings cannot support a claim of predecision prediction.

## 3. Shared Data Objects

Each assertion retains source location, speaker, polarity, time, role evidence and local party/contract/debt/event identities. A role is an identity attribute: BUYER cannot replace a specific within-case buyer ID.

| Independent state | Examples | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Source statement status | COURT_FOUND, ALLEGED, DISPUTED | How the source presents the proposition |
| Field state | KNOWN, MISSING, AMBIGUOUS, CONFLICTED | Whether extraction resolves the required value |
| Machine mapping state | AUTO_ACCEPTED, PENDING, REJECTED | Whether mapping passes current machine checks |
| Human review state | UNREVIEWED, REVIEWED_SUPPORTED, REVIEWED_REJECTED | Whether independent source review supports it |

COURT_FOUND + AUTO_ACCEPTED + UNREVIEWED means the machine identifies a passage as a court finding without human verification. It does not mean expert confirmation.

Research views may use explicitly marked machine mappings and propagate that status. Independent evaluation and views requiring human confirmation admit only records meeting their requirements. Preservation, display, retrieval eligibility and formal computational admission are separate decisions.

**Types, strict equivalence and families.** PAYMENT has amount, date, contract and debt parameters. Two members of that type need not have interchangeable amounts, statuses or debts. Strict-equivalence clusters require identical full canonical signatures under an explicit abstraction policy; unresolved relevant qualifications prevent equivalence. Families organize subtypes or related types without permitting substitution. SENT_NOTICE and RECEIVED_NOTICE are distinct subtypes in NOTICE_EVENT. Cross-case instance IDs remain distinct.

## 4. A: Definition Mapping, Use Checks and Source Repair

### Inputs and outputs

Inputs are contextual source assertions and a versioned definition registry. Cards contain type ID, meaning, parameter slots/types, important distinctions, exclusions, conditions on aliases, positives, difficult negatives, context and version. Outputs are canonical instances, type mappings, residual qualifications, use conditions and traceable revisions.

### A1. Initialization and mapping

1. Open extraction on development material preserves quantifiers, negation scope and shared events without forcing all facts into old categories.
2. Retrieve similar sources by action, argument types and context, then draft cards. Publish initial cards after source review and mark candidate cards separately.
3. For a new assertion combine exact aliases, BM25 and vector retrieval, initially examining at most 20 cards. Unknown context is not an automatic hard exclusion.
4. Check source support, conflicts and unknowns slot by slot. Canonical text must neither invent information nor omit important qualifications. Edits distinguish preservation, correction, meaning-preserving rewriting and unsupported-assertion exclusion. Allow at most one automatic repair per item and retain its original.
5. Accept a machine mapping only when a uniquely supported most-specific definition applies. Two applicable definitions without a reviewed inclusion relation remain ambiguous. Add parameters or a candidate type when the language cannot express the source.

Basic type recognition differs from eligibility for a use. A clear payment with unknown debt may map to PAYMENT while remaining unavailable to a debt-specific query.

### A2. Computational effect of unresolved qualifications

A residual qualification stores its source span, owning assertion/group, potentially affected fields/semantic positions and scope-review status. Positions include proposition validity, polarity, statement status, identity, contract, debt, amount, settlement target and time.

Every query atom, operator and legal bridge declares required reads. The compiler computes their transitive dependency set. FULL_SETTLEMENT(payment, debt) must read debt attribution, settlement target and statement status even if debt is not an output.

Use u is blocked exactly when a relevant unresolved qualification r affects a required dependency of u. Required fields and view-specific statuses must also qualify.

Unknown scope defaults to blocking its assertion. If ownership remains unclear after splitting, block potentially affected members of the original assertion group. An LLM's unsupported claim that a qualification is irrelevant cannot unblock it. Narrowing/removing a block requires reviewed source analysis or an approved parsing/projection rule.

Example: the court finds full payment, but only for D2. Until the latter phrase is parsed, full=true cannot discharge D1. An approved projection may retain that payment occurred without assigning a debt. A separate complete source for D1 payment remains usable while the residual stays attached to the first record; local uncertainty need not block the entire case.

Explicit contrary evidence is a conflict, not merely UNKNOWN. C reads both sides to form arguments. An existential query needs at least one fully eligible witness and must still disclose relevant conflicts; finding support cannot hide counterevidence.

### A3. Review selection and patch acceptance

Initially allocate one quarter of review effort randomly and three quarters to conflicts/boundaries. Random sampling begins with source paragraphs, including zero-extraction, excluded and high-confidence items, to expose omissions and systematic misreading. The targeted queue prioritizes conflicts with reviewed distinctions, multiple memberships, difficult boundaries and new expressions; within a tier favor diverse problems affecting several dispute families.

Allow only evidenced instance corrections, alias-scope adjustments, added parameters, definition splits and reviewed merges. A new version must repair at least one confirmed error or missing distinction without regressing previously correct applicable reviewed examples. Among passing versions choose the smallest change. Conflicting review answers remain disputed; do not broaden a definition to force agreement.

After release, remap instances and recompute use conditions and strict signatures. Higher average model confidence cannot replace acceptance checks. Examples used for repair are no longer independent tests.

Shared definitions provide reuse standards; repeated pairwise comparison is reserved for boundaries. This reduces redundant judgments and supports revision, but a bad card can propagate widely, so random source auditing remains necessary. Similarity retrieves candidates; it does not establish equivalence.

## 5. B: Executable Relations from Real Fragments

### Inputs, outputs and default search

Input is a frozen A version. Outputs are a relation library G with witnesses/failures and a smaller descriptive dictionary S selected from G. G stores executable relations with explicit review grades. S serves overview/compression, not all legally important relations.

Default search generalizes real fragments, then repairs queries under fixed meanings. LLM proposals supplement language and descriptive concepts. Short two-atom enumeration receives an initial 10% execution budget to cover omissions. Broad WARMR enumeration is not the main route: exhaustive search in a language missing debt/time relations cannot recover those relations. Sampling also misses structures, so searched scope and supplementation budgets must be recorded.

### B1. Seeds and safe language

Sample connected 2–4-atom fragments within contract/request scope. Real objects, events or sourced relations establish connections; shared case_id, type names or amounts do not. Initial resource caps are 50 fragments per family and 100 pairs per bucket.

Bucket by predicate, argument types, status and atom count. Typed anti-unification of compatible pairs replaces differing constants with variables, reuses variables for identical constant pairs, and preserves shared relations and explicit equality/inequality constraints. Different temporary IDs do not prove distinct real objects. Accidentally repeated names do not become universal constants.

Queries are typed ASTs compiled to relational joins, never arbitrary model code. Initial limits: six expanded base atoms, six object variables, four date/numeric variables, two derived-definition levels and no recursion. Permit registered comparisons, explicit inequality and sourced negation. Without complete-set evidence, do not compute complete performance or total amounts.

Deduplicate by enumerating type-preserving variable renamings, sorting atoms/constraints and choosing the least serialization. This resolves renaming equivalence, not arbitrary logical equivalence. Output-slot positions, states, constants and guards participate.

For each source group the LLM may propose at most eight items with natural-language meaning, signature, query AST, source positives and difficult neighbors. Compositional macros are abbreviations. Missing observed relations request A supplementation; new descriptive operators require registration and validation. Evaluative concepts such as valid, reasonable or unlawful belong in C.

### B2. Execution and repair under a fixed intent

An intent card states the meaning, indispensable conditions, inputs/outputs, sourced positives, explicit negatives and unknown examples. Maintain separate candidate beams for different intents; broad payment-and-notice co-occurrence must not displace full payment after receipt on the same debt merely through coverage.

Execution returns `(unit, binding, source_ids, intermediate_bindings, failed_guards, blockers, conflicts, review_grade)`, where unit is a case/request/stage. Preserve all witnesses; count support at most once per unit and separately by dispute family. Distinguish timeout, unknown fields, ineligible binding and no recorded witness after exhaustive search. None automatically implies real-world negation.

Prefer real near-counterexamples differing in one binding, state or condition. The program determines whether the query matches; independent source review determines whether that match fits the intent. Synthetic contract substitutions, state flips and missing-date examples test software only, not real support.

Patches may unify/separate variables, add evidenced constraints, repair status scope, add missing parameters, replace registered operators or split definitions. Allow at most three rounds per intent, eight children per parent and twelve distinct retained structures. Reject type/source/scope/protected-distinction violations first, then reduce reviewed false positives and missed positives without regressing correct answers. For equal semantics prefer smaller changes, shorter expansion and lower execution cost. Coverage is only a later criterion within the same intent.

Changing intent requires a new version and review, without inherited labels. Stop when evidence for repair or budget is exhausted and retain unresolved reasons.

```text
G = empty
seeds = generalize(sample_real_connected_fragments(A_frozen))
add bounded source-grounded LLM candidates and short-query supplements
for intent in fair round-robin order:
    beam = canonicalize(intent.programs)
    repeat at most 3 rounds:
        compile types, scopes and semantic dependencies
        execute, retaining bindings, blockers and conflicts
        compare outputs with source-reviewed intent examples
        archive eligible programs and their review grades in G
        edit only the diagnosed locations under the same intent
        keep up to 12 admissible distinct programs
        stop if no admissible improvement
freeze G and evaluate on untouched confirmation cases
```

## 6. B's Descriptive Dictionary: Minimal MDL Implementation

Minimum description length (MDL) asks whether one relation definition plus repeated parameterized calls is shorter than individual records. It measures descriptive reuse, not legal force or predictive value.

Version one uses nonoverlapping base records while allowing shared entities. G witnesses may overlap; S's compressed stream reconstructs each base record exactly once. Calls may share contracts, debts and parties. Snor-style repeated fact coverage and joint MILP selection are deferred: they add complexity outside the main research question. Relations excluded from S remain in G.

Fix D to one A release. Minimize `L(H) + L(S) + L(D | H,S)`. The common paid header H includes versions, source documents/fixed archives, units, entity/literal tables, base types and fixed operators. Derived definitions cannot be hidden in a free header.

A concrete code may use gamma(n+1) for nonnegative integers, length-prefixed UTF-8 strings and fixed-width table IDs determined by table size. Coding conventions and constant tables remain fixed across comparisons.

| Encoded object | Required content |
|---|---|
| Dictionary | Full used AST, constants, parameter types, guards and derived-dependency closure |
| Individual record | Type, record ID, all arguments, state, source, qualifications, residuals and review fields |
| Pattern call | Pattern ID, covered record IDs, all reconstruction bindings and residual fields |
| Unit header | Unit ID, record count and token count |

Existential variables may be required to decode even when not outputs and must be charged. Date comparisons do not generate original dates for free. Reconstruct by record ID with no omissions/duplicates and require `decode(encode(D,S)) == D`.

Inner covering begins with individual records and repeatedly chooses a nonoverlapping call that most reduces full data length, including count-header changes. Require at least one bit saved. Definition cost belongs to the outer objective; the first call need not repay the entire definition.

The outer loop tries adding one unselected pattern or deleting one selected pattern, recomputing cover and total length each time. After round-trip validation, accept the greatest net saving. Stop without positive gain or at budget. Compare legal conversion of the previous cover with reconstruction and retain the shorter. This local approximation can miss jointly beneficial additions and is not globally optimal.

## 7. C: Rule Acquisition, Interpretation Bridges and Argumentation

### C1. Source packages and tests

Inputs include legal text, definitions, exceptions, cross-references, jurisdiction/time/procedural scope and judicial use. Follow identified references with visited tracking and a budget; unavailable provisions remain gaps. Completeness is relative to identified references, not all potentially relevant law.

Using A's registry and G, jointly propose:

- RuleCard: premises, conclusion, modality, scope, exception policy and priority.
- BridgeCard: additional requirements connecting descriptive relations to legal conditions; actual receipt alone does not establish valid notice.
- FactorCard: a supporting/weakening reason with target issue, direction, qualitative degree, interactions and source.

Each component points to its source. Explicit rules, reconstructed judicial reasoning and cross-case hypotheses stay separate; statistical majorities do not grant legal authority. Deemed receipt is a legal rule and never overwrites A with an actual-receipt event.

Allow at most three interpretations and three sourced repair rounds each. Independent tests cover necessary conditions, exceptions, party/contract/debt binding, deadline triggers, scope, explicit negation and missing information. The compiler checks types/bindings; legal review assesses normative meaning. Expected answers cannot come from the tested program itself. Different outputs establish disagreement, not which interpretation is correct.

Patches correct only evidenced conditions, quantifiers, modalities, bindings, references or exception directions. Replay reviewed cases and check coverage of source components so deleting hard conditions cannot inflate pass rates. Unreviewed substantive interpretation, bridges, priorities and exception policies execute only in explicitly marked research mode.

### C2. Unknown exceptions

| Reviewed policy | Execution | Output when exception is unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Supported exception attacks application | Build a defeasible argument when ordinary premises hold; qualified exception arguments attack the rule application | Default argument may be accepted while exception uncertainty remains explicit; no inference that the exception is absent |
| Exception must be explicitly excluded | Require supported explicit negation as a premise | Without it, this rule cannot apply |
| Policy unresolved | Exclude from the formally executable package | Await interpretation; do not silently choose a default |

Construct eligible exception arguments and attacks before computing acceptance. Do not require an exception already accepted before permitting its attack. Missing premises for one route do not establish the opposite conclusion. An IN conclusion dependent on a default must display that dependence, without becoming an unconditional fact or being silently erased as no conclusion.

### C3. Computation and first-version limits

Use restricted ASPIC+: finite objects, no support recursion, defeasible factual projections and legal rules; attack cycles are allowed. Source occurrence and admissibility as fact remain separate, and contrary assertions can coexist. Undercuts target rule applicability; rebuts target contrary defeasible conclusions. Reviewed policies distinguish attacks from effective defeats.

Use last-link priority only when the relevant argument has a unique explicit last defeasible rule. It is not the last executed line of code. General rule-set comparison, complex strict legal closure, burden shifting and nested obligations are outside the fragment. Retain their cards and unsupported status without rewriting semantics.

Compute grounded labels on the complete finite defeat graph: start empty; arguments whose defeaters are all OUT become IN; arguments defeated by IN become OUT; iterate to stability, leaving others UNDECIDED. Graph-construction timeout means incomplete computation, not a final result on a graph missing attacks. Labels describe the given rules and inputs, not proof that the legal interpretation is correct.

Factors that only supply reasons output `ReasonFor/ReasonAgainst(issue, factor)`. Without a reviewed bridge they do not establish elements or victory. Partial payment may support willingness to perform but not full settlement. Qualitative strength does not invent numeric weights, and factors do not automatically add together.

## 8. Running Example: One Contract, Different Debts

All facts and rules below are fictional execution examples. Case Alpha concerns K1/K2; K1 has D1/D2.

| Record | Source statement | Use for full payment after receipt on K1/D1 |
|---|---|---|
| n1 | Court finds notice sent May 1 and received May 4 by the designated recipient, explicitly identifying K1/D1 | Eligible; receipt date is May 4 |
| p1 | Court finds full payment May 10, only for K1/D2; latter phrase initially unparsed | Initially blocked; after resolving D2, explicitly fails the query |
| p2 | Court finds partial payment on K1/D1 on May 11 | Fails full-settlement condition |
| p3 | Party alleges full K1/D1 payment May 12, without a court finding | Preserve allegation; exclude from court-found view |
| n2/p4 | K2/D3 notice has dispatch date only, plus full payment | Receipt unknown; deadline cannot be computed |
| p5 | Separate court-found K1/D1 settlement May 13; revocation unspecified | Complete descriptive witness, unaffected by p1's residual |

A attaches p1's residual to debt attribution and settlement target. B's faulty `NOTICE(n,c1,d1) AND PAYMENT(p,c2,d2)` can combine unrelated records. Under the fixed intent of settling the same debt after notice receipt, unify contract/debt variables, require receipt, settlement target and source state, then compute elapsed time.

```text
FullPaymentAfterReceipt(c,d,n,p,delta) :=
    ReceivedNotice(n,c,d,t_receive)
    AND FullSettlement(p,c,d,t_pay)
    AND delta = t_pay - t_receive
    AND delta >= 0
```

Alpha's witness is `(K1,D1,n1,p5,9 days)`. Dispatch-based counting gives 12 days but violates the definition. If Beta receives notice May 5 and settles the same debt May 12, it supplies another witness. Do not invent compression savings or call two examples a validated universal pattern.

The fictional law requires actual delivery to the designated recipient with clear contract/debt identification. Full settlement of the same debt within calendar-day difference 0–10 after valid receipt prevents termination solely for that unpaid debt. Legally revoked payment is an exception; absent qualified revocation evidence, the rule applies by default.

C checks recipient/identification bridges, then timing and full settlement. Alpha's K1/D1 argument may be IN under the reviewed default while disclosing unknown revocation. K2/D3 lacks the receipt date. Under a different policy requiring explicit exclusion of revocation, Alpha also lacks a premise. The result concerns one termination ground, not the whole case outcome.

## 9. Versioning, Outcome Association and Implementation Order

B/C may request extraction or definitions for source content absent from the language. A changes only from sources and abstraction policy, not to fit outcomes. Initially recompute fully within a small domain: successful/pending mappings, failed bindings, formerly pruned candidates, semantic relation tests, the MDL baseline and legal arguments. A changed D prevents attributing raw code-length differences solely to an algorithm.

Freeze types, abstraction policy, queries, use dependencies, coding and rules before confirmation. If confirmation errors inform changes, those materials become exposed development data.

After outcome-label validation, investigate group-specific patterns within training and compare regularized logistic regression on single facts versus facts plus relations using dispute-family inner splits. Candidate selection, regularization and length limits remain inside training; final test data are not used for repairs. No recorded witness is a documentary feature, not real-world negation. Partial success and procedural dispositions are not forced into failure labels. Association is neither causation nor legal authority.

Implementation order: shared identity/provenance/status and definition cards; A's use dependencies and reviewed repair; B's bounded queries, real fragments and local editing; C with a small reviewed source set; finally G/S selection and coding round trips. Complete one issue before expansion.

Defer dedicated fine-tuning, all-member EQ/MILP, broad enumeration, overlapping-record coding, complex multipattern swaps, possible worlds, general strict legal logic, automatic factor weights and prediction without reliable labels. These may be useful later but are not simultaneous prerequisites.

## 10. Hypothesis and Falsification

ClassMATe, GraphRefine, RHDA and ADVENT motivate definition revision, extraction repair, executable local feedback and language acquisition respectively. Their tasks and supervision differ; mechanisms transfer as proposals, not their reported scores.

Primary hypothesis: with identical models, sources and review budgets, purpose-specific blocking and local repair retain more correct witnesses and reduce cross-contract/debt, state and deadline errors relative to ignoring qualifications or discarding whole assertions.

Compare these three strategies on erroneous use, retained correct witnesses, unresolved rate and expert time. Source reviewers uninvolved in repairs judge key distinctions. Reject-all cannot win on precision alone; model self-tests are insufficient semantic evaluation. Coding round trips and tiny exhaustive covering comparisons validate implementation/approximation cost, not legal effectiveness.

If scope assessment is unreliable, fall back to assertion-level blocking. If LLM local edits have no budget-matched gain, retain real seeds and deterministic execution. If S does not help reading, G/C remain independently useful.

Relative to the PDF, retain embedding/LLM retrieval and judgment while specifying admission, revision and source replay algorithmically. FP-Growth remains usable on correctly constructed items; gSpan remains a same-graph structural-search comparator. Neither supplies missing descriptive language or legal bridges. ANCO-HITS's exact version remains unverified, so no superiority is claimed. Research differences must be demonstrated in semantic dependencies and repair benefits, not mere module combination.

See [literature and final choices](文献依据与最终取舍.md) for reading scope. Correct execution, correct source interpretation and cross-case generalization are three separate acceptance questions.
