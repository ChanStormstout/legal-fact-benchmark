BHAGWATI J [COURT_RECORDED]: The central legal question is whether, prior to the 1929 enactment of Section 65-A of the Transfer of Property Act, a mortgagor in possession had the power to grant a permanent lease of the mortgaged property so as to bind the mortgagee.

High Court of Judicature at Patna [COURT_RECORDED]: The High Court negatived the contention regarding constructive res judicata. It considered whether Wazir Narayan Singh had power to grant the permanent lease, concluding the lease did not render the Bank's security insufficient under Section 66.

High Court of Judicature at Patna [COURT_RECORDED]: The High Court held the lease was valid and not affected by the Plaintiff's mortgage, decree, or execution sale, and accordingly dismissed the Plaintiff's suit. The Plaintiff obtained leave to appeal to the Privy Council.
