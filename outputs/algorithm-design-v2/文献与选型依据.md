Review date: 2026-09-29. Recent-work window: 2024-09-29 through 2026-09-29, with relevant classics retained. “Full-text checked” refers to the listed sections, not every proof, reproduction or exhaustive coverage. These are Codex reading records; Pro self-reports remain separate original records.

The [broader register](../project-wide-research/独立文献核查.md) covers legal factors/KGs, ontologies, retrieval, ILP, hypotheses, argumentation, formalization and query-guided cleaning. This round focuses on nearest mechanisms that affect implementation choices. Comparator proposals remain historical, not authorization to enhance the current plain-LLM baseline.

## Research judgment

Use explicit existing algorithms rather than rename an LLM-checking workflow. A needs admission rules, B search/set objectives and C declared semantics. Combining provenance, relations and symbolic execution is insufficient novelty. Potential contributions concern useful output, cost and reuse under legal status/identity constraints, subject to experiments.

Advantages may disappear once alternatives receive identical information. Every claimed benefit therefore retains a rejection experiment. Literature supports choices, not local data quality, expert agreement or end-to-end gains; source audits/mechanism checks remain separate from published scores.
