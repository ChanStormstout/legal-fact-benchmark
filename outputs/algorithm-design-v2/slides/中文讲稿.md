# Core Algorithm Design: Eight-Slide Speaker Script

Historical proposal, September 29, 2026. Approximately 10–12 minutes. Examples and rules are fictional; real-data superiority is unverified.

## 01 Three Different Problems

A makes expressions reusable as fact categories while preserving case-specific identity and statement status. B discovers recurring relational combinations across cases. C builds arguments from sourced, reviewed rules and handles rebuttals. Point to the direct A-to-C line: B assists retrieval/comparison but is neither the only factual entry nor a source of legal rules. The present corpus is judgments, so version one supports retrospective discovery and argument reconstruction, not predecision prediction. The full project also includes factors, elements, requests and issues; these slides focus on algorithms connecting those layers.

References: Canonicalizing Legal Facts (1).pdf; supplied team assignment and full Evidence-to-Conclusion pipeline. Whiteboard excluded. Design §§1,6.

## 02 Information A Must Preserve

The fictional K1 statements may share PAID_FULL as content, but allegation and court finding belong to different canonical statement classes. Status must affect computation rather than remain unused metadata. A payment family organizes classes without permitting substitution. Preserve parties, contract, time, source span and speaker for each instance. Extract/check slots, retrieve with BM25/embeddings, then test mutual entailment of each abstract-signature condition in both orders before EQ. Missing information, disagreement or added behavior blocks merging. Nondelivery and refusal to deliver illustrate similar wording without equivalence. The original PDF already requires provenance/status; implementation and merge decisions are the proposed work, not those requirements as novelty.

References: PDF pp2–6; EDC https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.548/; Design §§2–3. Hypothetical text, not an actual case.

## 03 Grouping and Greedy Selection

Nodes are abstract signatures with shared status/context; edges are checked EQ judgments. Missing edges are either unchecked or non-equivalent, distinguished in storage but both unavailable for co-clustering. Greedy ab leaves c and d singleton; joint ac/bd retains two checked relations without chaining unchecked edges. Binary zij represents same-cluster membership; non-EQ pairs are zero and triangle constraints enforce a partition. Maximize within-cluster EQ edges. Components up to 80 nodes receive 60 seconds; larger ones use full-admission greedy fallback. Recompute affected old/new components after evidence changes and version the result. This is a conditional graph-objective advantage, not improved language judgment. Compare greedy on the same EQ graph and the complete PDF representative-checking pipeline, EDC with shared schema, Cequel and LSCK. Retain greedy if extra solving does not improve correct reuse. Coral marks the example selection, not an established contribution.

References: Design §3; Bansal et al. Correlation Clustering https://doi.org/10.1023/B:MACH.0000033116.57574.95; Cequel https://arxiv.org/html/2504.15640v2; LSCK https://arxiv.org/html/2601.11118v1. Graph is a constructed mechanism example.

## 04 Generating and Checking Relational Patterns

Initially K1 is paid and K2 undelivered. Co-occurrence in one case is not a same-contract witness; repeating K in the query makes the join fail. A later K1 nondelivery finding supplies a witness. Allegation and court-found views run separately. Begin with one atom and extend by one sharing an entity/event variable. Canonicalize variables/atom order and execute typed joins. Initially use 2–4 content atoms and at most six variables. Count one support per case/request/stage, with dispute groups separately tracking independent recurrence and a fixed denominator. Added conjunctions only shrink witness sets, supporting pruning of valid known-infrequent prefixes. Unknown counts/timeouts are not zero. gSpan already preserves structure, so give it the same typed graph, identities and states and compare cost/coverage rather than claiming exclusive relational ability.

References: Design §4.2–4.3; WARMR https://www.cs.helsinki.fi/u/htoivone/pubs/dmkd99.pdf; gSpan https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~xyan/papers/gSpan-short.pdf. Hypothetical example.

## 05 Selecting a Pattern Dictionary

Redundant patterns waste reading effort. Dictionary calls retain b, s and K bindings; uncovered facts remain literal. Integer programming jointly chooses entries, witness calls and literals to minimize definition, invocation and residual bits. Fix field/ID widths, charge overlapping macros fully and reconstruct every atom. Discovery requires witnesses in at least five dispute groups; selected entries require actual use in at least three groups. These are different constraints at different stages. Version one permits one call per pattern/unit to limit long-case dominance; removing that cap is an ablation. At 300 seconds retain the best feasible solution and report the gap. Optimality guarantees no worse bits than greedy only for the same candidates/code, not legal value. Compare KG-MDL, SLIM/Snor, maximum coverage and same-code greedy. Change the objective or use greedy if small savings reduce expert utility. The three displayed calls are illustrations, not measured support or code lengths.

References: Design §4.4–4.7; KG-MDL https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12908; SLIM https://eda.rg.cispa.io/pubs/2012/slim-smets%2Cvreeken.pdf; Snor https://eda.rg.cispa.io/pubs/2025/snor-vanleeuwen%2Cvreeken.pdf.

## 06 C: Explicit Argument Computation

Extract conditions, exceptions and scope from law, obtain expert review, compile to shared predicates and build arguments with source/rule IDs. A source record states what the document says; defeasible projections produce object-level facts so contrary records coexist and rebut. In the K1 example, A supports no extension and B supports extension; projections mutually rebut. R combines A with other premises, while r2 uses extension to attack that application of r1. The red path abbreviates r2 visually; implementation retains its node. With equal priority, grounded iteration from empty leaves R unresolved. This is neither voting nor model credibility selection. Use reviewed partial priorities and the documented last-link elitist comparison. Undercuts are not blocked by conclusion priority; rebuts directly target defeasible subarguments. The package is finite, function-free and has a positive-dependency DAG, though attacks can cycle. Construction limits prevent certified conclusions from incomplete graphs.

References: Design §5; ASPIC+ tutorial https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1080/19462166.2013.869766; ANGRY https://arxiv.org/html/2508.10976v1. Diagram assumes equal-priority conflicting projections.

## 07 Unknown Is Neither Automatic Negation nor Universal Refusal

The running example lacks K1's due date; payment plus nondelivery cannot fill that legal premise. Distinguish reviewed rule policies. If explicit exclusion of extension is required, missing evidence prevents an argument. If ordinary conditions permit default application and extension attacks it, an argument can exist without an extension record, without inventing a no-extension fact. Source-based policy must be fixed before the case, not chosen to obtain a conclusion. Unreviewed policies do not execute. Conflicting extension arguments remain unresolved as on the previous slide. Traces include sources, projections, rule version, attacks, status and gaps. The hypothesis is fewer omitted conditions/arbitrary decisions, not guaranteed legal truth. Reuse a mature ASPIC+ engine supporting the same semantics; retain a simple checklist when equally effective.

References: Design §§5.3–5.6. All legal conditions on this slide are illustrative, not jurisdiction-specific legal rules.

## 08 Selection and Evidence Needed

These are implementable, falsifiable choices combining established algorithms. A's conditional advantage is strict admission plus joint legal-merge competition, with all additional checking costs counted. B's hypothesis concerns binding, explicit language and redundancy reduction; equally informed gSpan/KG-MDL may match it. C uses mature formal semantics; the challenge is faithful compilation of sources, rules, exceptions and priorities. Start with one issue. Separate development/discovery/confirmation by related dispute groups and separate reviewed-input from automatic-input experiments. Evaluate coverage, correct output and cost together; singleton-only or reject-all cannot win through zero errors. The local inventory contains 6,954 mixed-domain records without ready gold. Small mechanism checks validate abstract/synthetic examples, not real-case performance. The meeting should settle protected legal distinctions, the first issue and expert-review arrangements.
