# Whole-document comparison plan

Arms: preserved V15 generic readers; new V16 specialist readers; preserved LW
same-model full-document adaptation. No method changes after freeze.

The unit of content assessment is a distinct source-supported relationship with
its speaker, stage, polarity, conditions and references. Match equivalent content
across output formats before counting coverage. Multiple redundant records do not
increase credit. One compound record may cover several relationships but must be
flagged if bindings or qualifications become ambiguous. Incorrect extraction must
not earn coverage merely because it contains the right keywords.

Read all source units and compare all available final content. Describe each item
as correct, partial, wrong, missing or not assessable, with source locations and
output IDs. Distinguish final source records from raw reading notes and compare raw
reading examples separately if final output is unavailable. Check whether errors
were corrected, merely rejected or retained. Do not turn technical failure into a
made-up semantic score.

Key questions: Does specialization reduce speaker/court confusion? Are conditions,
examples and burdens kept correctly scoped? Does reconciliation repair actual
cross-role disagreements? Does finer extraction preserve all important content?
Does the extra computation deliver useful content beyond V15 and LW?

No prewritten case-specific answers are put into model prompts. Current case is
exposed development data. The comparison is descriptive; no significance or general
method superiority will be claimed from a single case or unequal call budgets.
