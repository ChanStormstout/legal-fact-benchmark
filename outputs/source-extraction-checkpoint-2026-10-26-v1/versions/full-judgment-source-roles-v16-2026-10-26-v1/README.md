# V16 specialist source-extraction comparison

## Scope and intervention

One frozen attempt on the complete judgment 1251841 using the existing Together
Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B configuration. This is document extraction, not outcome prediction
or a downstream legal report. No source, reference label or corrected intermediate
answer is supplied beyond the same original judgment used by V15 and the saved LW
adaptation. No new training, retrieval, default switch, commit or push.

Three blind readers specialize in facts/entities, procedural scope/court attribution,
and legal rules/conditions/burdens. Each gets the full original and the same focus
units. Roles have distinct questions, not merely different expert names. All role
records become equal-status reconciliation targets. The existing reconciliation
compares them with originals; final review cannot edit them. There is no voting,
free-form debate, legal gold or program-authored semantic acceptance.

OUTSIDE_ROLE means substantive material outside one reader's assignment. It is not
NON_SUBSTANTIVE or a claim of global coverage. The reconciler checks joint coverage
against the complete original, including omissions shared by all three readers.
No model reading may change the frozen source. Exact duplicates may be aliased;
distinct speakers, stages and effects must remain separate.

## Execution and preservation

V15 continues unchanged. V16 waits for its final accounting, subtracts actual
accounted spending (including unknown reserves) from the same remaining six-case
$6 authorization, then takes the shared API lock. It runs no other cases. The
30-call V15 and 41-call V16 use the same model parameters, source and transport;
180-second socket timeout and identical technical retry policy remain unchanged.
Estimated V16 cost based on recent returned reading calls is about $0.25-$0.50,
not a promised charge or a separate authorization. A no-usage failure retains the
preconfigured reserve and stops the case; no semantic rerun until success.

V16 performs 33 specialist readings, four source reconciliations, four final reviews.
The saved LW run uses a modified same-model adapter, not native repository/model
performance. Its completed extraction and technical validation failure both remain.

## Comparison and interpretation

Compare the same source relationships, not raw record counts. Primary descriptive
outcomes are source-supported coverage and materially incorrect relationships;
report both together. Review speaker/participant identity, family relationships,
property/time, court/stage, claims versus findings, legal conditions, exceptions,
burdens and authority treatment. Record omitted source content separately from
unsupported content, compound records, technical failure and internal review status.
A short or highly rejected output is not automatically accurate or complete.

All source units must be considered, not only previously noticed errors. Preserve
partial output on failure; semantic assessment is unavailable for missing output.
Costs, calls, wall time and completion are reported separately. V16 has more calls
than V15; an observed difference cannot isolate role specialization from extra
computation. This is one exposed development case, not generalization evidence.
Final model acceptance is not the external assessment and not human legal gold.

Offline cached replay checks the complete 41-stage interface, source preservation,
role isolation, coverage representation and unresolved-only reviews. It does not
measure the new roles' actual quality. Live results reside in private/run-v1.
