## Candidate rules (OPEN_TEXT; not executable)

**R1** A misdescription may be treated as irregularity where identity is not doubtful; a change in the identity of property sold is different.

Restricted to identification from the actual decree/certificate particulars; never merge objects by similar names alone.

[IK-522414:L137](source.html#IK-522414:L137), [IK-522414:L138](source.html#IK-522414:L138), [IK-522414:L139](source.html#IK-522414:L139), [IK-522414:L144](source.html#IK-522414:L144), [IK-522414:L145](source.html#IK-522414:L145)

**R2** The plot 1060 conclusion rests on matching area, khata and boundaries plus absence of a rival plot 160 in that khata.

Case-specific judicial assessment reconstruction; not a universal cadastral matching algorithm. Other plots have separate mortgage issues.
