# Sadashiv Shyama Sawant(D) Th:Lrs &Ors vs Anita Anant Sawant on 22 February, 2010

How can a landlord seek recovery when a third party forcibly dispossesses the tenant, and why is tenant joinder not invariably necessary?

## Candidate rules (OPEN_TEXT; not executable)

**R1** A landlord retaining legal possession through a tenant may maintain Section 6 recovery against a third party who forcibly dispossessed that tenant.

Maintainability only. Section 6 time limit, consent, due-course-of-law and government exclusion remain; title is not adjudicated by this rule.

[IK-161859415:L151](source.html#IK-161859415:L151), [IK-161859415:L152](source.html#IK-161859415:L152), [IK-161859415:L153](source.html#IK-161859415:L153), [IK-161859415:L155](source.html#IK-161859415:L155)

**R2** Tenant joinder may be desirable but is not an absolute prerequisite for the stated landlord Section 6 suit.

Do not translate not necessary into always irrelevant; retain the concrete third-party dispossession setting.

[IK-161859415:L157](source.html#IK-161859415:L157), [IK-161859415:L158](source.html#IK-161859415:L158), [IK-161859415:L159](source.html#IK-161859415:L159)
