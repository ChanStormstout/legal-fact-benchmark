# Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd vs Sri Sriman Narayan & Anr on 9 July, 2002

Why did the Court reject the High Court interim restoration order without finally deciding contractual revocation or possession at trial?

**Status:** Codex-assisted draft; no independent reference or legal approval.

## Attributed propositions

**P1 — PROCEDURAL_RECORD / Supreme Court procedural account**

The suit sought interim protection of the petrol pump possession and business; the challenged orders included restoration.

[IK-74028:L70](source.html#IK-74028:L70), [IK-74028:L71](source.html#IK-74028:L71), [IK-74028:L81](source.html#IK-74028:L81), [IK-74028:L89](source.html#IK-74028:L89)

**P2 — PARTY_CLAIM / Plaintiff as reported**

The plaintiff asserted continuing possession until forcible dispossession on 29 September 2000 after suit was filed.

[IK-74028:L72](source.html#IK-74028:L72), [IK-74028:L73](source.html#IK-74028:L73)

**P3 — PARTY_CLAIM / Defendant as reported**

The defendant asserted takeover on 22 September 2000 and delivery to the second defendant.

[IK-74028:L73](source.html#IK-74028:L73), [IK-74028:L74](source.html#IK-74028:L74)

**P4 — LOWER_COURT_FINDING / Trial court as reported**

The trial court relied on the listed documents and accepted the 22 September takeover account, finding no strong prima facie case.

[IK-74028:L75](source.html#IK-74028:L75), [IK-74028:L76](source.html#IK-74028:L76), [IK-74028:L80](source.html#IK-74028:L80)

**P5 — LOWER_COURT_FINDING / Andhra Pradesh High Court as reported**

The High Court regarded termination as prima facie illegal and the plaintiff as in possession when suit was filed, granting interim relief.

[IK-74028:L85](source.html#IK-74028:L85), [IK-74028:L86](source.html#IK-74028:L86), [IK-74028:L87](source.html#IK-74028:L87), [IK-74028:L88](source.html#IK-74028:L88), [IK-74028:L89](source.html#IK-74028:L89)

**P6 — TARGET_COURT_FINDING / Supreme Court, interim stage**

The target Court recorded the conceded change from proprietary firm to partnership and the contractual revocation clause; its view of entitlement to act was expressly prima facie.

[IK-74028:L117](source.html#IK-74028:L117), [IK-74028:L118](source.html#IK-74028:L118), [IK-74028:L119](source.html#IK-74028:L119)

**P7 — TARGET_COURT_FINDING / Supreme Court, interim stage**

The Court criticized failure to address contrary trial reasons and whether damages would adequately compensate refusal of interim relief.

[IK-74028:L122](source.html#IK-74028:L122), [IK-74028:L123](source.html#IK-74028:L123), [IK-74028:L124](source.html#IK-74028:L124), [IK-74028:L125](source.html#IK-74028:L125)
