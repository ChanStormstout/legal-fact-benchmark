## Candidate rules (OPEN_TEXT; not executable)

**R1** Juridical protection from wrongful dispossession does not by itself establish lawful entitlement to receive property after requisition and expiry of a lease.

Target factual conjunction: State possession, expired lease without renewal, restoration stage. Do not generalize to forcible eviction of an occupant.

[IK-840688:L183](source.html#IK-840688:L183), [IK-840688:L184](source.html#IK-840688:L184), [IK-840688:L185](source.html#IK-840688:L185), [IK-840688:L186](source.html#IK-840688:L186)

**R2** Liberty to mention cannot reopen a decided matter; the Court nevertheless considered the merits under Article 136 in this procedural setting.

The procedural criticism and merits disposition coexist; neither deletes the other.

[IK-840688:L139](source.html#IK-840688:L139), [IK-840688:L140](source.html#IK-840688:L140), [IK-840688:L142](source.html#IK-840688:L142), [IK-840688:L160](source.html#IK-840688:L160), [IK-840688:L162](source.html#IK-840688:L162)
