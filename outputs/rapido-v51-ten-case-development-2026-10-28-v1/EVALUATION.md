# Evaluation contract and reference validity

R=correct important relations / frozen reference relations. Each important relation must preserve material actor, object, time, procedural attribution and legal condition. Incidental names, redundant paraphrases and typography are not independent recall requirements. P=supported distinct substantive assertions / actual substantive assertions; assertions are deduplicated consistently across similar texts. H=100*2*R*P/(R+P). Serious errors are reported separately and are not automatically erased by a higher aggregate score. An incomplete but improved report can be better; zero errors is not a prerequisite for improvement.

The report actually delivered is evaluated. Proposal-only edits and hypothetical corrected reports receive no credit. Correct conditional conclusions and justified unknowns may earn credit; unsupported certainty and uninformative avoidance do not. Technical report absence hasR/H 0 and undefinedP; missing annotation of an existing report remainsnull, not 0. All ten cases and all five predefined endpoints remain in the table.

Source-only references are built from allowed CASE/LAW and the question, apart from report grading. They do not authorize use of the withheld target disposition, external memory, extra law or a known correct intermediate answer. The grader sees anonymous final text, permitted originals, and the appropriate reference boundary; method maps, intermediate plans and old scores are not grading evidence. Grounding quotes, source/report hashes, raw model decisions, parent corrections and unresolved issues are preserved. Mechanical integrity checks establish identity and quotation, not semantic truth.

Reference provenance is not uniform: eight references have separate source-only design and design-review evidence plus parent source checks. The 89764 reference is a 31-item provisional parent split; the 1914817 reference is single-pass Web design with parent checks, without an independent design audit. These remain explicit limitations. Older assertion inventories for 89764 and 1489900 were generated with reference hashes only, not the later reference items; unchanged source/question/report hashes permit reuse, not a claim of fresh item-informed grading. Some recovered artifacts cannot be proven identical to their unreadable first responses; this provenance remains visible in the archived scores.

The parent knew method identities during parts of adjudication. This is source-grounded MODEL reference evaluation, not fully blinded independent legal-expert validation or human gold. Same unchanged report text has shared units and labels, including ambiguous claims. Source-label sensitivity ranges are not statistical confidence intervals. Missing-data identification bounds are not observed scores.

The complete A0 assertion axes for 406910 and 1388066 are unavailable. TheirH and total serious-error inventories staynull. Do not extrapolate the eight fully observed paired cases to all ten, particularly because 1388066 has a negative observedR difference.

No confirmatory hypothesis test was performed. The old cohort influenced design. A later claim of significant independent superiority requires a frozen task/reference/cohort, prespecified pairedR/H tests and multiplicity handling, fixed sample/stop rules, and evaluation on disputes not used to develop the method. This publication does not declare that objective achieved or alter its definition.
