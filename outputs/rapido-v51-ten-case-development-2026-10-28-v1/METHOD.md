# Executed method and comparator definitions

A0 receives the permitted raw CASE/LAW text and ordinary question in one call. No helper stages, examples, retrieval, decomposition, critic, self-review or edit are added. Actual A0 requests and responses are included; this publication does not regenerate A0.

P makes one source-plan call and one complete-draft call. C0 is the actual single-call complete-writing draft used by E, with its own writing instruction; C0 is not A0. E adds independent localization and an editor. F inherits the actual P plan and draft, then makes one editor call directly against the complete allowed originals, draft and fallible plan. It receives no reference labels, A0 answer, parent-corrected plan or ideal intermediate answer.

The F editor emits local replacement/insertion proposals. Deterministic code checks literal locations and applies accepted patches while preserving other text; mechanical acceptance does not certify legal meaning. Failed patches remain rejected, not silently hand-corrected. Six F reports are byte-identical to P. Four changed reports contain 15 applied and 4 rejected proposed edits.

Model: Together Qwen/Qwen 3.8-Flash. The frozen method assigns 4096 output tokens to planning,6144 to drafting and 6144 to editing, totaling 16384; A0 has one 16384 maximum-output call. Equal allocated output caps do not mean equal input tokens, calls, latency or cost. Inherited successful calls are not repeated. The original plan timeout in 236012 is retained.
